Our Story

Grown from one garden, one guest at a time.

Green Haven began in 2018 as a single acre and a folding table. Today we host proposals, birthdays, baby showers and corporate retreats across six landscaped acres — but the promise hasn't changed: every detail, from the linen to the last string light, is handled so you don't have to.

Our mission is simple — give people in Nairobi a place to slow down, gather, and celebrate outdoors without lifting a finger. Our vision is to be East Africa's most trusted name in outdoor experiences.
